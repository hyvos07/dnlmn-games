How to play

Join the numbers to make a 2048 tile.

  • Slide the board. Swipe on a phone, drag with a mouse, or use the arrow keys / WASD. All tiles move together.
  • Match equal numbers. Two matching tiles combine when they slide into each other.
  • Keep some space. A new tile appears after each move that changes the board. The game ends when no moves remain.
2+2→4

Tip: keep your largest tile in a corner. Reach 2048 to win, or keep going for a bigger score.
