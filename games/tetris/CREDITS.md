[TETR.IO](https://tetr.io/) and its [handling guide](https://tetrio.github.io/faq/mechanics.html) informed the solo-mode structure, configurable handling and presentation. This is an independent implementation. Rules and compatibility details are in README.md.

Music is synthesized locally with Web Audio; there are no downloaded music files, Nintendo/Game Boy soundtrack recordings, or copied modern score engravings. The sources above establish the traditional tune's identity and history; their downloadable arrangements are not bundled.
