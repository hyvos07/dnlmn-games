# Credits — Typing Fortress

## Original content

Fortress and creature meshes, landscape geometry, shader code, crest artwork, animation effects, Rune Bastion lore, curated English word list, and Web Audio synthesized music/effects were created for this game. No third-party models, texture packs, dictionary datasets, or audio recordings are used. Thumbnail and preview are recorded from real gameplay.

The battlement-and-rune SVG favicon is original vector artwork created for Typing Fortress.
